# Bloomhaven Flower Creation Bible v1.0

This document defines the standards for every flower added to Bloomhaven.

The goal is to create a collection that is educational, visually beautiful, botanically authentic, and mechanically interesting.

Every flower should feel like it belongs in both a real botanical garden and the world of Bloomhaven.

## Design Philosophy

Flowers are the heroes of Bloomhaven.

Players should immediately recognize the real-world inspiration while still appreciating Bloomhaven's stylized interpretation.

Every flower should encourage:

- Discovery
- Collection
- Education
- Hybridization
- Business decisions

Every flower should answer these questions:

- What makes this flower unique?
- Why would I grow it?
- What hybrids could it produce?
- What customers would love it?
- What makes it visually memorable?

## Artistic Standards

Every flower should follow Bloomhaven's Art Bible.

Characteristics:

- Modern cozy pixel-art influence
- Flat readability
- Strong silhouette
- Botanical accuracy
- Handcrafted feel
- Rich but simple color palette
- Clean value separation

Avoid:

- Hyper realism
- Cartoon exaggeration
- Fantasy redesigns of common flowers
- Excessive visual effects

Flowers should be immediately recognizable.

## Required Deliverables

Every new flower must include the following.

## 1. Full Botanical Profile

- Scientific Name
- Common Name
- Flower Family
- Native Region
- USDA Hardiness Zones
- Bloom Season
- Sun Requirements
- Water Requirements
- Soil Requirements
- Height
- Width
- Pollinator Information
- Interesting Historical Fact
- Bloomhaven Lore Entry

## 2. Soil Temperament

Each flower receives one primary soil preference.

Examples:

- Well-drained
- Moist
- Rich Organic
- Sandy
- Rocky
- Clay Tolerant
- Acidic
- Neutral
- Alkaline

These affect:

- Growth
- Hybridization
- Customer requests
- Greenhouse bonuses

## 3. Native Zone

Every flower belongs to one native region.

Examples:

- North American Prairie
- European Meadow
- Mediterranean
- Japanese Woodland
- Alpine
- Desert
- Wetlands
- Mountain Forest

This creates:

- Educational value
- Regional collections
- Future district compatibility

## Growth Stages

Every flower requires four complete illustrations.

Each stage must remain recognizable.

### Stage 1: Seedling

- Small leaves emerging
- Simple silhouette
- Recognizable leaf shape

### Stage 2: Young Plant

- Stem growth
- Leaf development
- Early branching
- No flowers

### Stage 3: Bud Stage

- Visible buds
- Almost flowering
- Distinct silhouette
- Creates anticipation

### Stage 4: Bloom

- Full mature flower
- Maximum color
- Maximum detail
- Signature appearance

This becomes the journal illustration.

## Color Varieties

Every flower should have three naturally occurring color varieties.

These should reflect real horticultural varieties whenever possible.

Examples:

Peony:

- Pink
- White
- Deep Red

Lavender:

- Purple
- White
- Pink

Coneflower:

- Purple
- White
- Yellow

Do not invent unrealistic colors unless the flower is magical or hybrid.

## Hybridization Profile

Every flower must support future hybridization.

Each flower receives:

- Primary Traits
- Secondary Traits
- Hidden Traits

Example:

Rose:

Primary:

- Romantic
- Elegant

Secondary:

- Fragrant
- Luxury

Hidden:

- Ancient
- Moon Affinity

Hybrid possibilities should feel logical.

Example:

Lavender + Rose -> Lavender Rose

Sunflower + Daisy -> Golden Star

Hybrids should inherit traits from both parents.

## Flower Statistics

Every flower should include:

- Rarity
- Sell Value
- Growth Days
- Fragrance: 1-5
- Beauty: 1-5
- Pollinator Appeal: 1-5
- Growth Difficulty: 1-5
- Hybrid Compatibility: 1-5

These values should be balanced across the collection.

## Customer Appeal

Define which customer types prefer this flower.

Possible audiences:

- Wedding
- Florist
- Hotel
- Restaurant
- Collector
- Researcher
- Festival
- Everyday Bouquet
- Luxury
- Native Gardening

This influences order generation.

## Pollinator Relationships

Every flower should identify:

- Bee Attraction
- Butterfly Attraction
- Hummingbird Attraction
- Other Wildlife

These can influence future pollination events.

## Seasonal Availability

Specify:

- Spring
- Summer
- Autumn
- Winter

Multiple seasons may apply.

This will later support seasonal farming.

## Educational Content

Every flower should include:

- One verified real-world botanical fact.
- One horticultural tip.
- One Bloomhaven journal note.

Example botanical fact:

"Peonies can live for over 50 years when properly established."

Example horticultural tip:

"Peonies dislike being transplanted once mature."

Example Bloomhaven journal note:

"Grandfather always planted peonies near the farmhouse because they reminded him of spring weddings."

## Illustration Standards

Each flower requires:

- Seedling Illustration
- Young Plant Illustration
- Bud Illustration
- Mature Bloom Illustration
- Three Color Variants
- Journal Portrait
- Inventory Icon
- Market Thumbnail
- Stained Glass Badge

The stained-glass version should simplify the flower into bold geometric shapes while preserving its recognizable silhouette. It is used for achievements, journal chapters, collection milestones, and premium UI elements.

## Animation Standards

Bloom stage only:

- Gentle sway
- Petal flutter
- Occasional pollen
- Subtle lighting response

Rare flowers may receive a very subtle magical shimmer.

Never overpower the natural beauty of the flower.

## Realism Rule

Bloomhaven is educational.

Every non-hybrid flower should closely resemble its real-world counterpart.

Do not alter:

- Petal count
- Leaf structure
- Growth habit
- Overall silhouette
- Color palette

Unless creating:

- Hybrid Flowers
- Legendary Flowers
- Moon Garden Flowers

Even then, the original species should remain recognizable.

## Flower Approval Checklist

A flower is not complete until it includes:

- Real botanical reference
- Scientific name
- Native region
- USDA hardiness zone
- Soil temperament
- Pollinator profile
- Seasonal bloom period
- Four growth stage illustrations
- Three authentic color varieties
- Full bloom illustration
- Journal portrait
- Inventory icon
- Stained-glass badge
- Traits
- Statistics
- Hybridization compatibility
- Customer appeal
- Educational fact
- Gardening tip
- Bloomhaven lore entry

## Final Test

Before approving a new flower, ask:

1. Would a gardener immediately recognize this flower?
2. Is the botanical information accurate?
3. Does the flower have a distinctive silhouette?
4. Does it add meaningful gameplay?
5. Does it create interesting hybrid possibilities?
6. Does it teach the player something real?
7. Does it feel unmistakably like Bloomhaven?

If the answer to any of these is "no," revise the flower before adding it to the game.
